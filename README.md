# Catch of the Day

A daily fish guessing game. Name it in 6 guesses.  
New fish appears every day at midnight EST.
If you have comments or suggestions. Please email me @justinsinger2001@gmail.com

1) 182 North American freshwater and saltwater fish. How often each one comes up is weighted by frequency of reporting, with a boost for famous  offshore fish to avoid esoteric species that most people do not know.
   
2) The he order is predetermined in advance for the next ~12.5 years, so the site will need updating at some point. No fish repeats until all
   182 have appeared, and similar looking fish from the same family are at least 10 days apart. I hope to add more species / other regions as well.

3) Your stats _should_ be saved in your browser.
