# Catch of the Day

A daily fish silhouette game. Name the fish in 6 guesses; a new fish appears every day at midnight Eastern.

- 182 North American freshwater and saltwater fish. How often each one comes up is weighted by research-grade
  iNaturalist sightings in the US (counts via GBIF), with a boost for famous offshore fish.
- The order is fixed in advance for about 12.5 years, so the site never needs updating. No fish repeats until all
  182 have appeared, and look-alikes from the same family are at least 10 days apart.
- Everything is in `index.html`. Stats are saved in each player's own browser.
