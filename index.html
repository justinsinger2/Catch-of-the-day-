    Catch of the Day is a free, Wordle-style daily game for anglers and fish nerds: name the mystery fish from its silhouette in 6 guesses. A new fish every day at midnight Eastern. No fish repeats until all 182 in the pool have had their day, and look-alikes from the same family are kept at least 10 days apart.
    Familiar fish come up more often: picks are weighted by research-grade iNaturalist sightings in the US (counts through GBIF), with a boost for famous offshore fish people rarely photograph alive. Sizes are typical adult lengths, and the drawings are simplified.

    How to play

    • Name today's fish in 6 guesses. Start typing and pick a fish from the list.
    • Each guess shows how close you are. Family turns green for the same family and yellow for the same broad group, like any shark or any reef fish. Water is fresh, salt or both. Size compares typical adult length, and the arrow says which way to go.
    • Miss 3 times and the fish shows its colors. Miss 4 and you get its family. Miss 5 and you get the first letter.
    • A new fish lands at midnight Eastern.
