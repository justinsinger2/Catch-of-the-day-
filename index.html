      Catch of the Day is a free, Wordle-style daily game for anglers and fish nerds: name the mystery fish from a blurry photo in 6 guesses. A new fish every day at midnight Eastern, and no fish repeats until all 182 in the pool have had their day.
      Photos come from iNaturalist and Wikimedia Commons photographers under Creative Commons licenses and are credited under each photo. Fish are weighted by US sightings on iNaturalist (counts via GBIF).

      How to play

      • Name today's fish in 6 guesses. Its photo starts very blurry and gets sharper with every miss. Start typing and pick a fish from the list. The blanks under the photo show how many words and letters its name has.
      • Each guess shows how close you are. Family is green for the same family and yellow for the same broad group, like any shark or any reef fish. Water is fresh, salt or both. Size compares typical adult length, and the arrow says which way to go.
      • Stuck? Tap Hint. The first hint sharpens the photo to slightly blurry, the next gives the family, and the last fills in the first letter. Hints don't use up guesses, but they show up when you share.
      • A new fish lands at midnight Eastern.
